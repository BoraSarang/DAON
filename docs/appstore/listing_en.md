## Full description
DAON is a **farming calendar** designed to sit on a kitchen or living-room shelf. Large type and a large calendar grid make it readable from across the room.

**Calendar**
- Solar and lunar dates together, with Korean public holidays and the 24 solar terms in each cell.
- Today is highlighted; move to the previous or next month.
- Tap a date to open a side panel with the solar term, farming tasks, holiday notes, and events.

**Weather**
- Current temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind, and rain chance.
- 3-hourly and 7-day forecasts, with rain, snow, and thunder shown as screen effects.
- Farming notes for snow, rain, wind, cold, heat, temperature swings, and dry spells.
- Keeps the last values when the connection drops.

**Clock and rotation**
- Choose a round analog, large digital, or combined clock.
- When idle, the screen rotates calendar → weather → clock. Set the dwell time for each screen.

**Events**
- Add events for any date in the date panel.
- If the tablet has a calendar app, its events are shown too (read-only).

**Settings**
- Text size (normal / large / extra large), 12/24-hour, °C/°F, language (English / 한국어).
- Search your neighborhood once to use its weather.
- Kiosk: screen pinning and optional start-on-boot (may be limited by the device).

**Privacy**
- No account, ads, or analytics.
- Location and calendar data stay on the device and are not sent to a developer server.
- Weather coordinates go to Open-Meteo; neighborhood searches go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
